11-5-26

this website is on its third visual revision, and fourth technical revision.
when i first made it i was using github pages to host. it had this gaudy yellow background with gaudier borders to seperate boxes of content. it had a big header that linked to places on the site (that i would copy and paste in each html file) and a little gif of a 3d cartoon model of me doing something relevant to the page. every page had an motp, a message of the page, and id have some joke about the page that id probably think was funny at the time and hate now.
i eventually moved to neocities, and with it redid the website. it still had pretty gaudy yellow boxes of content with these huge thick orange borders, but the background was this space pattern with yellow stars. i had a bar on the left of the page to navigate around that i built with javascript on every page and (notably...) at the bottom of it were two of this ghost gif i found on gifcities where the ghost was just freaking out flailing its limbs around, and needless to say pretty funny.
i eventually moved OFF of neocities cause i never really used the social features and started to get scared that people might find my website. i was a weird teenager, im over it now. but anyways i went back to github pages again and redid my website again, i desaturated the background and box colors and made the borders tiny. its what the site looks like now at the time of writing, ill try to remember to rewrite this if i ever change it...
...which i just might reasonably do because my fourth revision of this website was completely refactoring the css and using python to make my own static site generator! i kept how it looks because right now i think its pretty slick, but everything is thrown together with code so itd be super easy to change it. oh and the art sidebar on the right is new to this revision.

"okaayyyy," you say, "but what does the title of this post mean?" well i was just about to get to that.

these are the two ghosts from the old sidebar i mentioned. the one on the left is named weemus, and im preeeeetty sure but not certain that the one on the right was named wharmo. weemus was always my favorite anyways.

there was also warlmoro, who i remember coming up with and thinking they were REALLY FUNNY at the time because their title was "the bastard child", but i dont remember where they actually were on the site. also, id officially like to redeem warlmoro, weemus and wharmo got married and warlmoro was legitimized.

thats a little off track but what i was getting at was this is a GOOD bit. this is a funny gif, and its fun when you learn you can hover over these characters and learn they have names!
every time i redid my website, including the most recent one where i didnt change how it looked, all the content was shifted around and rewritten and a lot of GOOD stuff is lost. i didnt archive any of it, maybe i could find files for my first and third revision on one of my hard drives but the neocities one is definitely lost because the files only existed on their servers for my now deleted site.
today at work i relistened to the creepcast reading of tales from the gas station, theres a character in it nicknamed marlboro and i heard that and thought it was funny and twisted it into warlmoro for a new ghost on my website that followed the w m convention. anyways not weemus, nor wharmo, nor warlmoro have crossed my mind in YEARS until just today, when i was thinking about this earlier i thought "oh i gotta make a BLOG about this" (so lame lol) and even that i almost forgot to do.
i guess im advocating for you to archive everything you ever do. its fun to look back on! my google drive is stuffed with stuff i made as a kid, i have dozens of unfinished godot projects in my godot folder (which i should really back up, i lost my oldest ones at some point), i even have every single piece of work i took home from elementary to high school.
write down your thoughts, keep a diary, make a public blog. make a god damn website and put EVERYTHING on it!!!!!!!!!
