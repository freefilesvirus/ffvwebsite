i dont remember at all what inspired me to make a gamemode, but that was my first attempt (that i actually COMPLETED!) at a gmod addon. i had so much fun and kept making them, which led to getting into game dev which led to so much wonderful stuff i have going for me right now. i had programmed (and i think at this point even used godot before) before but getting into making these was probably the most significant domino to where i am now.
